# User Journeys

Status: built on localhost, not deployed. See ADR-011 to ADR-016, ADR-023 and ADR-025 (2026-10-01, which revised J-004 and J-005 and added J-007 to J-009). Journeys J-001 to J-006 were rewritten on 2026-10-01 for the shop-window model: the site takes no orders and sells no ebook. J-003 (direct ebook purchase) is retired while every book is in KDP Select.

For each journey record entry, preconditions, steps, first value, exit and recovery.

| ID | Person | Trigger | Successful outcome | Recovery path | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| J-001 | Reader who finds harni.uk first (browse) | Opens Home or Books | Picks a reader path or group chip, or the "Most popular" feature, and reaches a book page in one click | A missing Amazon link hides the button and never shows a broken one | Scenario test required |
| J-002 | Reader who wants to buy | Opens a live book page | Taps "Buy on Amazon" (Kindle) or "Get the paperback" and lands on that book's Amazon page in a new tab. Amazon sells, delivers and refunds | Amazon link missing: button hidden, "Coming soon" shown | Scenario test required |
| J-003 | Reader who wants to know more about a book | Opens a book page | Reads the band (cover, title, subtitle, Published, Kindle Unlimited and page tags), About this book, Who it's for and the advice note, meets the author box and sees four more books. The "Inside you'll learn" lists stay on Amazon behind a link | Not applicable | Scenario test required |
| J-004 | Reader who wants to hear about new books (join the list) | Taps "Join the list" | Ticks the consent box, submits an email and sees a thank-you with a reference. A copy of the sign-up arrives by email (reference, date and time, address, the exact consent wording and version, what to expect, how to unsubscribe). Consent is single step, with no confirmation link. To stop emails the person replies to unsubscribe, and the operator honours it before the next mailing | Form error shows an inline message. If only the copy fails the page says to keep the reference and not resubmit. No copy arrives: spam-folder hint, then email `subscribe@harni.uk`. Mail unavailable: the page says so and gives a direct address | Local mail tests PASS; real inbox NOT TESTED |
| J-005 | Reader with a question (contact) | Opens Contact | Fills the contact form (name, email, optional subject, message, Privacy and Terms acknowledgement), sees a thank-you with a reference, and gets a copy by email with what happens next (a reply is aimed for within five working days, not guaranteed). Or emails `info@harni.uk`, or follows Instagram, TikTok or the Amazon author page | Form unavailable or fails: honest message, input kept, direct address shown as text. Network doubt: check the inbox before retrying | Local mail tests PASS; real inbox NOT TESTED |
| J-006 | Reader checking the rules (read legal) | Opens a legal page from the footer | Reads Privacy, Cookies, Terms or Delivery and refunds, then returns to Books | Cookie choice can be changed from the footer link | Scenario test required |
| J-007 | Reader who wants to read what others say (read reviews) | Opens Reviews | Sees optional "Kind words" quotes (only with written permission, none today), then follows a link to read or write a review on a book's Amazon page. Cover cards cover all 13 books. No Amazon review text or star rating is copied | Nothing to show: the page says so honestly and still offers the Amazon links | Unit tests PASS; page not browser-tested |
| J-008 | Person looking for events or wanting to invite Harni | Opens Events | Sees upcoming events or, today, an honest empty state with join-the-list, a past events section, enquiry guidance and "what to include" for an invitation | No events: empty state. No booking exists until agreed in writing | Unit tests PASS; page not browser-tested |
| J-009 | Visitor who wants a break (play the game) | Opens Pier Run | Plays a bright seaside run: tap or swipe, or Space, Up, W to jump (hold to go higher), Down or S to duck, P or Escape to pause. Collects books and hearts, uses power-ups, ends with Play again or See the books. Sound is off by default. Nothing is saved unless the player ticks "Remember my best score on this device" | Browser blocks storage: the game still plays. "Delete saved score" removes the saved best | Unit tests and Lighthouse PASS; real phone, audio, screen reader NOT TESTED |

Specify loading, empty, success, validation error, service error, permission denied, offline and session-expired states where applicable. The direct ebook purchase journey (hosted checkout, download link) is removed while all 13 books are in KDP Select (ADR-023, BR-002). For this release: Events and Reviews have an empty state; Thank you is the success state for the mailing list; there is no sign-in, so permission and session states do not apply.
