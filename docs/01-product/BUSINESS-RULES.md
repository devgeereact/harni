# Business Rules

Status: the real-catalogue build runs on localhost only and is not deployed. See ADR-011 to ADR-016 ADR-023 and ADR-025 (2026-10-01). ADR-023 makes the site a shop window for the 13 books on the owner's Amazon author page. ADR-025 adds BR-013 to BR-017 and supersedes BR-007.
